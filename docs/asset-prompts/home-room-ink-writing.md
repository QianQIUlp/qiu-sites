# home-room-ink-writing · Desk close-up

Goal: a Light/Dark pair of the real computer close-up shown after clicking 「写作」 ("Writing"); the monitor shows a clean black screen so code can overlay the article list.

## Canvas

- 1672×941, 16:9; a push-in on the same room as the overview.
- The black screen's inner area is about x=30.25%–56%, y=32.8%–60.3% and must not be covered by objects or highlights.

## Prompt

> A clean low-ISO photographic push-in to the exact left writing desk. The same monitor,
> lamp, notebook, pen, keyboard, mouse, cup, chair, window, blinds, curtains and snow-white
> wall remain spatially coherent with overview. Monitor screen is blank black and nearly
> square to camera at the required coordinates. Generate registered night (lamp on) and
> soft overcast day (lamp off) versions; independent real material textures and smooth gradients.

## Negative

> readable text, generated UI, moved objects, beige/stucco wall, paper grain, shared embossed
> pattern, AI noise, film grain, fake HDR, CGI, painterly style, watermark, baked vignette

## Checklist

- [ ] Same objects and materials as overview
- [ ] Light/Dark strictly registered; the lamp is lit only in the Dark asset
- [ ] Black screen aligns with `.room-monitor-surface` and has no readable text
- [ ] White wall and all materials are clean and real, with no cross-material noise
